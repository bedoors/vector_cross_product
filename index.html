Vector cross product explorer

The cross product A×B is a third vector, at right angles to both. Its magnitude is AB sin θ, the area of the parallelogram the two vectors make, and its direction comes from the right-hand rule. Drag an arrow tip to change a vector, or drag anywhere else to turn the view.

The unit vectors î, ĵ and k̂ cross in a cycle: î×ĵ = k̂, ĵ×k̂ = î and k̂×î = ĵ. Going the other way round gives a minus sign, and a unit vector crossed with itself gives 0. Multiplying out A×B part by part with these rules gives the determinant formula.
